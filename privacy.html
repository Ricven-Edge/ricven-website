Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Overview

Ricven Edge respects your privacy. This policy explains how the Ricven Edge website and our digital products, including Daily Quiz & Challenge and RichInsights, collect and use information. The exact information handled can depend on the features you use and the app version.

Information our apps collect

Daily Quiz & Challenge and RichInsights collect operational and usage information to provide, maintain, secure, and improve their features. Depending on the app and features used, this information includes activity and interaction data, session and usage metrics, crash reports, performance diagnostics, account-related information, app settings, and device-related information needed for the services to work.

How information is used

Information is used to provide app features and services, support accounts and user settings, understand app usage, diagnose crashes or performance problems, maintain security, and improve the products.

Third-party services

Our apps use third-party services to support authentication, analytics, crash reporting, storage, advertising, content delivery, and backend infrastructure. These providers may process information as needed to provide their services. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and the configuration of each app. Product-specific disclosures should be consulted for details about the providers used by a particular app or version.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children unnecessarily. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our services, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with our services. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as our website and products develop. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision. Please review it periodically for changes.

Contact us
